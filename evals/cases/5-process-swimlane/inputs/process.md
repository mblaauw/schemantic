# Building permit applications (fictional)

1. The applicant submits the application in the online portal.
2. The intake officer checks that it is complete; if not, the applicant gets two weeks to add documents.
3. The case handler assesses the plan against the zoning plan and asks the welfare committee for advice.
4. The welfare committee gives advice within three weeks.
5. The case handler drafts the decision; the team lead signs it.
6. The portal sends the decision to the applicant and publishes it.
Systems: the online portal, the case management system (used by intake, case handler and team lead), the
document archive.
