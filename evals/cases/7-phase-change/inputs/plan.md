# Warehouse programme, phase 2 (fictional)

Today: orders arrive in the ERP; the ERP prints pick lists; pickers work from paper; the carrier portal is used by
hand to book shipments.
Phase 2: a warehouse management system (WMS) is introduced. The ERP sends orders to the WMS. Pickers use a handheld
picking app. The WMS books shipments with the carrier through an integration. Paper pick lists are removed.
The carrier portal stays for exceptions. Planned for phase 3, not now: slotting optimisation in the WMS.
