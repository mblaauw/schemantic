# What to do with a donated tool (Oakbridge tool library, fictional)

First look at whether the tool is safe to use. An unsafe tool that has a power cord goes to the repair café, where a
volunteer electrician looks at it; any other unsafe tool goes to recycling.

A safe tool: ask whether we already have three of them. If we do, sell it at the spring fair. If we do not, it goes on the
shelf to lend, once the desk has labelled it.

Nobody has decided yet who pays when the repair café cannot fix a tool.
